## 3. My favorite literary masterpiece

For **The Odyssey by Homer**, provide:

- A brief overview in your own words, or review the sourced background in the draft.
- Your personal reasons for choosing this work.
- The themes, characters, or lessons that most influenced your appreciation.
- Personal connections and realizations you gained from reading it.
- The translation or edition you read, if you know it, so quotations can be credited accurately.

## 4. Summary of learning

Describe the knowledge, concepts, and skills you gained throughout Great Books. Consider works from different historical periods and cultures; the literary lenses you used; literature's connection with human experiences, beliefs, and societies; and your development in critical thinking, interpretation, and analysis.

## 5. Personal reflection

Use your own experiences to answer these five questions:

1. How has your understanding and appreciation of literature changed during the course?
2. What challenges did you face when reading and analyzing the works, and how did you overcome them?
3. Which literary lens most influenced your interpretation, and why?
4. What did you realize about life, society, culture, or human nature?
5. How will you apply what you learned in your personal, academic, or future professional life?
